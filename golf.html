The High Country has more good golf than people expect. These are the courses we know and play, roughly in order of distance from the cabin. Mountain courses book up on fall weekends, so call ahead for tee times.

Mountain Aire Golf Club

West Jefferson, NC — about 20 minutes from the cabin

Our home course and the closest to the cabin. Dramatic elevation changes (some tee boxes drop 200 feet), long-range views, and a layout that's fair for all skill levels. Relaxed atmosphere and reasonable rates.

Owner's Tip: Mountain Aire is very accommodating — they'll even rent an extra cart for non-golfers. My wife and friends often ride along the front nine with drinks in hand, enjoying the views. By hole 10, they usually decide to ride the full 18 — it's like a mountain hike without the walking.

mountainaire.com · (336) 877-4716 · Directions

Jefferson Landing Club

Jefferson, NC — about 20 minutes from the cabin

A Larry Nelson–designed course that runs along the New River valley — flatter and more walkable than the mountain courses, with well-kept fairways and a nice clubhouse with dining. It's semi-private, so call ahead to check public tee time availability.

visitjeffersonlanding.com · (336) 982-7767 · Directions

Boone Golf Club

Boone, NC — about 30 minutes from the cabin

A classic Ellis Maples course and the go-to public 18 in Boone. Well maintained, walkable valley layout, and popular with locals — open roughly April through November. Good option if you're already spending the day in Boone.

boonegolfclub.com · (828) 264-8760 · Directions

Olde Beau Resort & Golf Club

Roaring Gap, NC — about 1 hour 15 minutes from the cabin

A hidden gem in the Blue Ridge Mountains. Expertly maintained with lush fairways, gorgeous landscaping, and challenging holes framed by sweeping vistas. As a frequent golfer, I can confidently say it's one of my all-time favorite places to play — and it's surprisingly affordable for what you get.

Owner's Tip: Make a day of it — after your round, take the scenic Blue Ridge Parkway route back to the cabin via Deep Gap. It adds to the experience with stunning mountain views and a peaceful drive.

oldebeauresort.com · (336) 363-3333 · Directions
