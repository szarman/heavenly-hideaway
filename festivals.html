🎶 Todd Summer Concert Series (Todd, NC – ~5 minutes away)

The Todd Summer Music Series is a free, family-friendly outdoor concert series held at Cook Memorial Park in downtown Todd. Concerts take place on select Saturdays throughout the summer, featuring a variety of musical genres. Attendees are encouraged to bring a chair or blanket to sit on. Well-behaved, leashed dogs are welcome. Food, including hamburgers, hotdogs, veggie burgers, chips, and cookies, is available for purchase.

2026 Concert Lineup:

  • June 13, 6–8 PM – The Lucky Strikes
  • July 4, 11:30 AM–1:30 PM – The King Bees (following the Todd Liberty Parade)
  • July 18, 6–8 PM – Wayne Henderson & Friends with Denim & Plaid
  • August 15, 6–8 PM – Sassafras
  • August 22, 6–8 PM – The Burnett Sisters
  • August 29, 6–8 PM – Jeff Little Trio

Owner’s Tip: Arrive early to secure a good spot and enjoy the community atmosphere. Don’t forget to bring cash for the food vendors.

🎉 More Boone Events & Festivals

Looking for even more ways to celebrate in the High Country? Here's what's happening in and around Boone in 2026:

  • 🎨 Art in the Park – A juried art show series held monthly from May through October.
  • 🎺 High Country Jazz Festival – June 10–14, 2026. A long weekend of jazz performances, dinners, and late-night jams across Boone venues.
  • 🎸 Boonerang Music & Arts Festival – June 19–21, 2026. A free downtown street festival with music on multiple stages, local beer, and kids' activities.
  • 🎭 Horn in the West – June 26 – August 8, 2026 (Tue–Sat, 7:30 PM). The nation's longest-running Revolutionary War outdoor drama celebrates its 74th season during America's 250th anniversary.
  • 🎇 Boone 4th of July Parade & Fireworks – Parade at 11 AM along King Street in downtown Boone, with fireworks that evening.
  • 🎶 Symphony on the Mountain (formerly Symphony by the Lake) – July 17, 2026. Now held at Appalachian Ski Mtn in Blowing Rock — an outdoor orchestra concert with fireworks, themed "Americana: Celebrating 250 Years."
  • 🎤 Antlers & Acorns: The Boone Songwriters Festival – September 24–26, 2026. Songwriters and storytellers perform in venues throughout town, with free and ticketed events.
  • 🚴 Blood, Sweat, and Gears – A challenging cycling event through the Blue Ridge Mountains, held each June.
  • 🎻 Doc Watson Day Celebration – Honoring the legendary folk musician with live performances.

🎶 West Jefferson Events & Festivals

West Jefferson, nestled in Ashe County, offers a delightful array of events throughout the year, celebrating music, food, and community spirit. Here are some highlights:

  • Backstreet Concert Series: Fridays, late May through summer 2026. Free live music every Friday evening at Backstreet Park, now in its 15th year — bluegrass, blues, and classic rock by local and regional artists. (Rain location: the Farmers Market shelter.)
  • Christmas in July Festival: July 3–4, 2026. A unique summer event that combines holiday spirit with summer fun — live music, food trucks, arts and crafts, and vendors throughout downtown.
  • Ashe County Farmers Market: Held weekly (Saturday mornings, April–October), the market includes fresh produce, artisan crafts, food vendors, and often live music — a lively spot for both locals and visitors.
  • On the Same Page Literary Festival: September 16–19, 2026. A four-day celebration of the written word with readings, workshops, and author events, mostly free.
  • Olde Time Antiques Fair: September 19–20, 2026. Downtown West Jefferson fills with antique vendors — free admission and parking.

For a full calendar of events, visit the Ashe County Chamber of Commerce Events Page.

🍂 Fall Favorites in the High Country

Visiting during leaf season? Two beloved festivals land on the same October weekend at the peak of fall color — and they're just a short scenic drive apart on NC-194:

  • 🐛 Woolly Worm Festival (Banner Elk) – October 17–18, 2026. Famous woolly worm races that "predict" the winter forecast, plus crafts, food vendors, live entertainment, and kids' activities.
  • 🌽 Valle Country Fair (Valle Crucis) – October 17, 2026. An authentic harvest celebration with handmade crafts, home-cooked food, and mountain music in one of the prettiest valleys in the North Carolina mountains.

Looking ahead: 2027 dates for these events are typically announced each spring — check the linked official pages for the latest schedules.
