The one rule

To change the temperature, use ONLY the WARM ▲ and COOL ▼ arrows.

Do not press any other button before or after setting it. JETS or LIGHT right after an arrow opens the filter menu. MODE after an arrow changes the heating mode (green box).

When you press an arrow, the set point temperature flashes. Keep pressing while it flashes to change it. After 5 seconds the display goes back to solid, showing the current water temperature. That is normal. Your setting is saved.

Setting the water temperature WARM ▲ COOL ▼

1. Press an arrow onceThe display flashes and shows the current set temperature (not the water temperature).
2. Press again while flashingEach press of WARM raises the setting by 1°. Each press of COOL lowers it by 1°.
3. Stop pressingAfter 5 seconds the display goes solid and shows the actual water temperature again. Done.

Range 80°F to 104°F (maximum). Heating is slow, so set it early and keep the cover on while it warms up. The reading is only accurate after the pump has run a couple of minutes.

Not heating? Check the mode WARM ▲ or COOL ▼ then MODE

How to tell: in Standard mode the display shows only the temperature. If it alternates between the temperature and Ecn (Economy) or SLP (Sleep), the tub is only heating during filter cycles and the water will stay cool.

  1. Press WARM or COOL once. The display starts flashing.
  2. While it is flashing, press MODE. The mode advances one step: Standard → Economy → Sleep → Standard.
  3. Repeat step 1 and 2 until Std appears on the display for a moment. The tub is now in Standard mode and will heat to the set temperature.

Jets JETS AUX

  • Press JETS once for LOW jets.
  • Press JETS twice for HIGH jets.
  • Press a third time to turn the jets off.
  • AUX runs the other jets: press once for on, again for off.

High and AUX shut off after 15 minutes. Low may run by itself now and then to heat the water. That is normal.

Lights LIGHT

  • Press LIGHT once to turn on the LED lights.
  • Press again to cycle through the color patterns.
  • Keep pressing until the lights turn off.

Lights shut off by themselves after 4 hours.

Please do not

  • Hold any button down. Short presses only, under 2 seconds. Holding a button can open settings menus.
  • Press MODE except as shown in the green box above.
  • Leave the cover off. Cover and latch the tub after every use.
  • Turn off the breaker or unplug the tub.
  • Bring glass into or near the tub.

Safety and display messages

  • Supervise children at all times. Limit soaks to 15 minutes at 104°F.
  • Pr on the display: the tub is restarting. Wait 5 minutes.
  • OH, OHS, HH, OHH: too hot. Do not enter. Remove the cover and let us know.
  • Ecn or SLP alternating with the temperature: the tub is not in Standard mode. See the green box above.
