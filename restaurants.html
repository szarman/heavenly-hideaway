These are the places we actually eat when we're at the cabin. West Jefferson (about 20 minutes) has a walkable downtown with several good options; Boone (about 25 minutes) has everything a college town should.

Boondocks Brewing

West Jefferson, NC — about 20 minutes from the cabin

A local favorite with two downtown locations: the Tap Room & Restaurant and the Brew Haus. Wide selection of craft beers and a diverse menu, often with live music and events.

Owner's Tip: Try their blackened shrimp tacos — they're some of the best I've had.

boondocksbeer.com · (336) 246-5222 · Directions

New River Brewing

West Jefferson, NC — about 20 minutes from the cabin

A rustic setting with amazing wings, burgers, and brews. Fresh, local flavors that fit the mountain vibe of the area.

Owner's Tip: Their pulled pork sandwich is tender and flavorful — a must-try.

newriverbrewing.beer · (336) 846-2739 · Directions

Black Jack's Pub & Grill

West Jefferson, NC — about 20 minutes from the cabin

A cozy downtown pub known for creative burgers and friendly vibes. A casual place to grab a beer and a great meal.

Owner's Tip: Build your own burger, and don't miss their wings.

blackjackspubandgrill.com · (336) 246-3295 · Directions

Dos Amigos

Boone, NC — about 25 minutes from the cabin

Need to feed a big group without breaking the bank? Dos Amigos sits just down Highway 194 as you enter Boone, with generous portions at great prices — a solid option for families or hungry hikers.

Owner's Tip: Don't miss their margaritas — excellent and surprisingly affordable. A reliable go-to when you're craving Mexican food.

Website · (828) 265-1674 · Directions

Macado's

Boone, NC — about 25 minutes from the cabin

Right on King Street in downtown Boone, with a vast menu and fantastic sandwiches. A great spot to refuel if you're spending the day in town.

macados.net · (828) 264-1375 · Directions

The Pedalin' Pig

Boone, NC — about 25 minutes from the cabin

Real North Carolina BBQ with slow-smoked meats and bold flavors. Brisket, ribs, pulled pork — it's all good.

Owner's Tip: The pulled pork is tender and delicious, and their variety of sauces means every bite is a little different.

thepedalinpig.com · (828) 355-9559 · Directions
