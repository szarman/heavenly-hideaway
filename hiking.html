Some of the best hiking in North Carolina is within a short drive of the cabin. These two are our favorites — one summit hike with big views, and one easy waterfall walk right off the Blue Ridge Parkway.

Elk Knob State Park

Todd, NC — about 30 minutes from the cabin

Elk Knob offers a rewarding 4-mile round-trip hike — 2 miles up and 2 miles back down — to one of the highest peaks in the High Country. The trail is rated moderate to strenuous, but it's wide, well-maintained, and safe for families. No steep drop-offs or technical sections, so it's a great choice for adventurous kids too.

Owner's Tip: Pack a lunch and enjoy a picnic at the summit. The panoramic views at the top are absolutely worth the climb, and it's usually not crowded even in peak season.

Cascade Falls (E.B. Jeffress Park)

Blue Ridge Parkway, milepost 271.9 — about 35 minutes from the cabin

Cascade Falls is a scenic, easy-to-moderate hike that follows a peaceful creek and leads to a beautiful multi-tiered waterfall. The loop trail is well-marked, gently sloped, and perfect for a quick nature walk or a relaxing family outing.

Owner's Tip: This is a great Blue Ridge Parkway stop — quick, refreshing, and photo-worthy. Bring a camera and enjoy the sound of the water as you descend to the viewing platforms.
