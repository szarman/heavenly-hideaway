The South Fork of the New River runs right through Todd, minutes from the cabin. It's one of the oldest rivers in the world, and its gentle current makes it perfect for tubing, kayaking, and fishing. Two local outfitters cover everything you need — or float it yourself with our DIY approach below.

RiverGirl Fishing Co.

Todd, NC — about 5 minutes from the cabin

RiverGirl is in the heart of Todd and rents tubes, kayaks, and bikes. The staff makes the process quick and friendly, and their tubing route is calm and scenic — the river winds gently through farmland and shaded mountain curves. Perfect for hot summer days.

Owner's Tip: In spring or fall when the water is cold, choose a kayak to stay dry and still enjoy the float.

rivergirlfishing.com · (336) 877-3099 · Directions

Zaloo's Canoes, Kayaks & Tubes

Jefferson, NC — about 20 minutes from the cabin

Zaloo's offers a different experience along a wider, deeper stretch of the New River. Their float route has fewer rocks, broader views, and holds up well when water levels are low. Great for a second-day float or if you want something more open and sunny.

zaloos.com · (336) 246-3066 · Directions

DIY Tubing

Owner's Tip: If you'd rather float on your own time, pick up cheap inner tubes and a 12V car pump at Walmart in West Jefferson. Launch from Green Valley Park and float 1.5–2 hours back into Todd. Use a two-car system (one at the park, one at the pickup spot) and you can float as many times as you want. This approach is scenic, peaceful, and a favorite among returning guests.
